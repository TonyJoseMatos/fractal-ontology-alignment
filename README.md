This framework marks a definitive break from the standard AI interpretability ecosystem. Instead, drawing from **Nick Cammarata's seminal 2026 work** (*"Neural Networks Are Already Clean"*), we interact natively with the network's internal architectural geometry.

This project maps the hidden lines of **Recursive Generalization** across deep layers to prove a paradigm-shifting truth: artificial systems handle macro-metaphorical abstractions flawlessly because their internal distinctions are fractal.

### The Cinematic Core: The Mr. K Allegory
By pulling weight logs from the network's non-linear layers, this engine tracks how a material crisis (the polarization of a **K-Shaped Economy** and its erased middle class) maps directly onto the eschatological ontology of **cosmic birth pains**.
